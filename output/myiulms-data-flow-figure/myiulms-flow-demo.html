Four quick answers before you sign in

Where does my sign-in go? Open answerClose answer

MyIULMS sends your registration number and password directly to iulms.edu.pk over HTTPS. It does not send your sign-in through a separate MyIULMS server.

What stays on my phone? Open answerClose answer

The active portal session and details needed to reconnect stay in app memory while you use MyIULMS. With Remember me, the app attempts to save your login in encrypted Android storage after a successful sign-in.

Does the app send my IULMS data somewhere else? Open answerClose answer

Your sign-in and academic requests go to the IULMS portal. GitHub is contacted only to check public app-update information.

What happens when I log out? Open answerClose answer

Logging out clears the active portal session. A saved Remember me login stays on the phone until a later successful sign-in with Remember me turned off.
