## Highlights / What's New (v1.3.0)

* **Sign in with full visibility** — The rebuilt sign-in screen says plainly that your credentials go over HTTPS to iulms.edu.pk only, with a trust chip that opens a short "How it works" sheet. The footer keeps the unofficial, not-affiliated notice, and the chip confirms with a subtle haptic.
* **Pick your class reminder time** — Reminders can now arrive 5, 10, or 15 minutes before class (10 by default) from the top-bar menu. A reminder never posts once class has started, and class-end notifications are replaced by a quiet widget refresh.
* **Attendance risk at a glance** — Every course shows how many absences remain before the 75% minimum, with Safe, Caution, and Danger chips and a 16-week projection from your timetable.
* **Transcript insights you control** — An optional unofficial CGPA excludes F, W, I, and XF attempts, with a one-time hint. The Courses & labs header also filters by any grade on your transcript or by Weak courses, each with a count.
* **Flagship interaction polish** — Haptics, branded pull-to-refresh, predictive back with a live Schedule preview, GPA/CGPA in the top bar as you scroll, progress reveals, and one consistent custom dropdown across the app.

## Fixes & Improvements

* **More dependable alarms** — The notification chain keeps a safety-net retry, logs skipped posts instead of swallowing errors, and surfaces exact-alarm access when Android needs it. Reminders stop at class start, including on devices with aggressive battery management.
* **Theme and system bars stay in sync** — Status and navigation bar icons now follow the in-app theme before the first frame and on every toggle, so a light app on a dark phone no longer shows white icons on a light background. Theme switching remains an instant snap.
* **Navigation resilience** — MainActivity is singleTop, so relaunching reuses the running screen instead of stacking a stale copy. Back from any tab returns to Schedule, and distant tab jumps no longer flash an intermediate tab.
* **Refresh that behaves** — Pull-to-refresh now works on error screens and all six lists, with one indicator for student-requested refreshes and quiet background syncs.
* **Reading and layout polish** — One-row attendance stats, segmented metric strips, 20dp course cards, 48dp controls, a filled Open voucher button with whole-rupee amounts and short dates, and 4dp progress bars. B+ now reads as Strong (green), matching the previous grading scheme.
* **Predictive back without the stutter** — The Schedule preview stays composed and draws only while the back gesture is in progress, so the first swipe no longer drops a frame.

## Data Ethics Statement

* **Direct to the portal.** Sign-in and every data request go over HTTPS to https://iulms.edu.pk. There is no MyIULMS server or database in the path.
* **Nothing collected in the background.** The app has no analytics, telemetry, or tracking, and does not upload portal data anywhere.
* **Credentials on your terms.** Remember me is opt-in. When enabled, credentials are encrypted on-device with an Android Keystore-backed key; when disabled, nothing is saved. Session cookies stay in memory, and signing out clears the session.
* **Student-initiated sharing only.** Result, schedule, and transcript images are created and shared only when the student starts the share action.
* **Unofficial and independent.** MyIULMS is not affiliated with Iqra University, and the academic data shown is the portal's own.
