# MyIULMS v1.4.0

## Executive Summary

v1.4.0 is the biggest interface release since MyIULMS launched. The four screens students use most — Weekly Classes, Exam Schedule, Attendance, and Results — have been redesigned around a single question: what do I need to know right now? Alongside the layout work, grade colors are now unmistakable in both themes, class reminders survive aggressive battery management, and a failed update check finally tells you why it failed instead of blaming your connection.

Everything still goes straight to iulms.edu.pk over HTTPS. There is no MyIULMS server, no analytics, and no tracking, and this release does not change that.

## Weekly Classes

* **A day at a glance** — The schedule now leads with the current day, with each class marked as live, coming up, or finished. The Live state appears once, without duplicate announcements for screen readers.
* **Today marker on the sticky header** — The day-of-week chips stick while you scroll, and today is marked so a long timetable never loses its place.
* **Selection survives refresh** — Pull-to-refresh and background sync keep the day you were viewing instead of snapping back to today.
* **Course code above the title** — Every row shows the course code (for example AIE331) directly above the course name, matching the portal's own labelling.
* **One duration format everywhere** — Classes and exams use the same compact duration style (1h 20m), replacing the mix of formats from earlier versions.

## Exam Schedule

* **Calendar-block cards** — Each exam is presented as a block with its date, day, time, room, and course, so the whole exam period reads as a timeline instead of a table.
* **Smart phases** — Exams in progress are highlighted, upcoming exams are listed in order, and exams that already passed are dimmed but still readable.
* **Compact spacing** — Card spacing was tightened so more of the exam period fits on a phone screen without losing breathing room.
* **Four-column share image** — The shared PNG export keeps a clean four-column layout for date, time, course, and room.

## Attendance Tracker

* **Four-tier risk math** — Every course is scored against the 75% attendance minimum using its own timetable: Safe (5 or more safe absences left), Caution (2–4 left), Danger (0–1 left, the F limit already reached, or 75% no longer reachable), and Unknown when the weekly meetings cannot be determined.
* **Planned sessions, not guesses** — Planned sessions are computed as weekly meetings × 16 weeks × sessions per meeting, and the F limit is 25% of that plan rounded down (16 of 64, 8 of 32). The last safe absence is one below the limit, because the absence that reaches it earns F.
* **Exact F-threshold countdown** — The single-line status pill states precisely how many absences remain before F, instead of a vague warning.
* **Exam ineligibility is explicit** — Once attendance drops below 75%, the course is marked as exam-ineligible, and absence counts stay unclamped afterwards so the real number is visible.

## Results

* **Semester GPA hero card** — A single card now leads the tab with the semester GPA, the average and highest course totals, and the exact count of A and A− grades, next to the semester label and course count.
* **Score ring course cards** — Each course is a card with a 56dp grade-colored ring showing the total out of 100, the grade badge, the grade points, and a full-width Mid / Quiz / Project / Final breakdown row.
* **Two-decimal GPA** — The hero and the top-bar GPA read to two decimals (for example 3.50), matching how the portal reports GPA.
* **A clearer subtitle** — The Results heading now reads "Semester marks & GPA".

## Theme & Accessibility Polish

* **Distinct amber C and red F** — Grade colors were rebuilt so each band is recognizable on its own: emerald for strong grades, ocean blue for average, golden amber for weak, and sharp red for critical. C and F are no longer easy to confuse.
* **Better light-mode contrast** — The old weak-grade brown sat too close to the critical red on white backgrounds. The new amber and red pair is clearly separated in light mode, with matching dark-theme variants.
* **One source of color** — The ring arc, the grade badge, and the grade-points text all inherit the same grade color, so a card never mixes signals.
* **Everything inherits the theme** — The redesigned screens keep full light and dark support and remain readable at font scales from 0.85x to 1.3x without clipping.

## Reliability & Fixes

* **Reminders survive aggressive battery management** — Class reminders now share one alert cycle between the alarm receiver and a WorkManager safety net, deduplicated by a handled watermark.
* **A fallback alarm keeps the chain alive** — While reminders are on, a concurrent 15-minute fallback alarm prevents a missing schedule cache from silently killing the reminder chain.
* **One clear system blocker** — When reminders cannot work, the Schedule tips surface only the current blocker (notifications, exact alarms, or battery optimization) instead of every possible cause.
* **Remember me is a real prerequisite** — Turning on reminders without Remember me keeps the toggle off, explains why in a snackbar, and hides the lead-time row until reminders are actually active.
* **Update checks explain themselves** — A manual update check now reports the real cause: GitHub's 60/hr rate limit (HTTP 403), a missing release (404), an unreadable response, a timeout, or an unreachable host. The old generic "check your internet connection" message no longer hides a rate limit behind an internet problem.
* **Automatic checks stay quiet** — Background update checks still fail silently, are limited to once per six hours, and never interrupt a student who is signing in.

## Technical & Compliance Notes

* **285 unit tests across 31 suites** — Schedule row states, exam phases, attendance risk math, results formatting, reminder cycles, and update-check messaging are covered; the full suite passes before release.
* **Version 1.4.0 (versionCode 20)** — Same signing identity as earlier releases, so it installs in place over v1.3.1 and keeps saved settings and Remember me credentials.
* **Direct to the portal.** Sign-in and every data request go over HTTPS to https://iulms.edu.pk. There is no intermediary server or database.
* **Nothing collected in the background.** No analytics, telemetry, or tracking; portal data is never uploaded anywhere.
* **Credentials on your terms.** Remember me is opt-in and encrypted on-device with an Android Keystore-backed key; session cookies stay in memory and signing out clears the session.
* **Unofficial and independent.** MyIULMS is not affiliated with Iqra University, and the academic data shown is the portal's own.
